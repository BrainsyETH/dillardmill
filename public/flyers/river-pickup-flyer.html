Pine Valley at Dillard Mill  ·  Davisville, Missouri

Leave the car.
They’ll come get you.

Three Steelville river resorts will pick you up right here at Pine Valley, put you on the water, and bring you back at the end of the day. No second vehicle, no shuttle logistics, no wet seats on the drive home.

No. 01
Lucky Clover Lakeside River Resort
Upper Meramec River
Canoe Kayak Raft Tube
Family-run resort on the upper Meramec with its own pick-up and drop-off service. Open roughly May through October.
888-404-9154 luckyclovercampground.com
No. 02
Garrison’s River Resort
Meramec River
Canoe Kayak Raft Tube
River shuttles run on the hour — 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 1 — so you can match your float to a slow morning or a long afternoon.
573-775-2410  ·  800-367-8945 garrisonscampground.com
No. 03
Huzzah Valley Resort
Huzzah Creek  ·  Courtois Creek  ·  Meramec River
Canoe Single Kayak Tandem Kayak Raft Tube
Floats all three waters daily, with trips from two miles up to twelve — the widest range of the three, and the closest to Pine Valley.
573-786-8412  ·  800-367-4516 huzzahvalley.com
Book the float when you book the cabin.
Call the resort directly and tell them you’re staying at Pine Valley on Dillard Mill Road — arrange pickup when you reserve. Floats are seasonal and depend on water levels, so confirm your trip and pickup time a day ahead.
